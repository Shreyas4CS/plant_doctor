How it works

One field, two kinds of brains

Coggy reads the same four sensors every second. What changes is how it decides to run the pump and the lights.

STEP 1

Connect Coggy

Pair the ESP32 over Bluetooth. Live readings appear right under the menu.

STEP 2

Write rules

In Automation, set rules like "if soil is dry, pump on" and the motor timings. Go live and test.

STEP 3

Teach for 5 days

In AI, farm by hand for five days. Coggy records every choice and learns the pattern.

STEP 4

Compare

Once both are tested live, the comparison table shows who saved water and kept plants healthy.

Meet Coggy

The brain wired into your field

Coggy is the ESP32 board in the middle of the field. It sends a reading every second over Bluetooth and switches the pump through the L298N motor driver.

The same board can run either brain. Here is how each one thinks about the pump:

Automation
if soilDry and time in 6:00–6:30:
  pump = ON
else:
  pump = OFF
# never changes by itself
AI (learned)
# found in 5 days of data
if raining: pump = OFF
elif soilDry and time < 7:30:
  pump = ON # 92% sure
else: pump = OFF

        How to play

        Your goal: find out how Automation and AI decide differently.

        1. Connect CoggyPress Connect Coggy and pick your ESP32. Sensor readings appear under the menu. No kit? Use Demo Coggy.
        2. Automation: write rulesAdd rules such as "if soil is dry, pump on" and a watering time. Then press Go live.
        3. Test Automation liveWet the soil sensor, splash the rain sensor, cover the LDR. Move the field clock. Watch what the rules do, then exit live mode.
        4. AI: farm for 5 daysSet the field clock, switch the pump and lights yourself and save at least 4 moments per day. On Day 3, make it rain at your watering time.
        5. Train and test AI liveFeed the 5 days to Coggy, read what it learned, then go live and try the same tests.
        6. CompareOnce both brains have run live, the Compare button unlocks the Automation vs AI table.

        End this session?

        Rules, the 5-day diary, the trained brain and all live results will be deleted from this device.

        Retrain from scratch?

        This clears all 5 days of the diary and Coggy's trained brain. Your Automation rules stay.
